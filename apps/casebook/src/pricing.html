Questions

Does my studying still work offline?

Yes. Casebook runs entirely in your browser on every plan. Once the app has loaded, drills, vignettes and the glossary keep working with no connection — and the tutor, once its model has downloaded, runs on your device too.

What happens to my progress if I cancel?

It stays where it always was — on your device. Cancelling returns you to the free tier; it does not erase what you have learned or your drill history.

Is the tutor sending my questions somewhere?

No. The tutor downloads a small language model once and runs it locally. What you type never leaves your computer, on any plan.

Can I switch which app Pro covers?

Yes. Pro covers one app at a time and you can change which one. If you are studying for more than one exam, Unlimited is cheaper than two Pro subscriptions.
