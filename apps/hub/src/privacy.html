Privacy

What is collected, and what is not.

This is a plain-language description of how these apps work today. Fill in the contact and jurisdiction details, and have it reviewed before you rely on it.

Your study data stays on your device

Drill history, answers, flags, scores and settings are stored in your own browser using local storage. They are not uploaded, not backed up to a server, and not visible to anyone but you. Clearing your browser data for this site erases them permanently — there is no copy elsewhere to restore from.

The AI tutor

The tutor downloads a language model once and then runs it entirely on your device. Prompts you type and answers it produces are processed locally and are never transmitted. The only network activity is the one-time model download, served by the model host's CDN.

If you contact us

The contact form sends the name, email address and message you enter, so a reply can be sent. That is used for correspondence only: it is not added to a marketing list, not sold, and not shared with third parties for their own purposes.

If you subscribe

Payment is handled by our billing provider. Card details are entered on their systems and are never seen by or stored on this site. We receive only what is needed to know that a subscription is active — an account identifier, plan, and status.
