About Casebook

Study that fits in your pocket and works anywhere.

Casebook is a free, offline study companion for the ASWB social work licensing exam. It pulls the exam's core content into one place — a glossary to learn from, spaced-repetition drills to lock it in, and full-length practice vignettes to test whether you can actually apply it under pressure — and it keeps working on a train, on a plane, or anywhere the signal drops.

Why I built it

Preparing for a licensing exam is stressful, and the tools for it are often expensive, cluttered with ads, or locked behind a subscription — none of which helps when you're anxious and just want to study. I wanted something clean, honest, and free: an app that respects your attention, runs on the phone already in your hand, and never sends your progress or your questions to anyone. This exam is a real gate in front of a career built on helping people, and I wanted the path to it to feel a little less lonely.

What's inside

  • 207 glossary terms across all eight ASWB content domains
  • 217 practice vignettes, each with the correct answer and the reasoning behind it
  • Drills that resurface what you miss and space out what you know
  • A tutor that runs a small AI model entirely on your device — optional, and private by design

Your privacy

Everything happens in your browser. Your progress, your answers, and anything you ask the tutor stay on your device — nothing is uploaded, tracked, or shared. The tutor downloads its model once, then works with no connection at all.

Honest limits

Casebook is a study aid, not official ASWB material, and the tutor is a small model that can be wrong. Verify anything you rely on against the current DSM-5-TR, the NASW Code of Ethics, and your jurisdiction's statutes.
